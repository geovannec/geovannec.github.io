Governance

Community moderation

There is no central moderator. Each community defines its own rules, moderators and policies — including minimum Proof-of-Work to post and owner review of testimonials.

Removal is by filter, not decree: moderators publish verdicts; your client decides what to show. Nobody — not even the project — can delete content from relays.

Web of Trust

Trust is computed from your network: direct friends, friends-of-friends and verified PoW are labelled on scraps and posts — a transparent signal, not a secret score.

Strangers outside the network are not silenced — they are marked as outside the network, so trust context stays visible.

Project transparency

ADRs document every architectural decision; the sprint plan is public; the changelog describes each release. Moderator recalls and appeals follow a public flow inside the community.

Funding

The project sells no ads, data or attention. Infrastructure costs zero by design (relays are public and interchangeable). There are no investors to please — only users to serve.
