Why Sovereign Social exists

2026-10-01 · Product

The communities that shaped the Brazilian internet — forums, scraps, testimonials — were shut down by business decisions. When Orkut closed in 2014, fifteen years of culture vanished in a press release.

Sovereign Social exists because that cycle doesn't need to repeat:

  • Your account is a key on your device. No e-mail, no password reset, no third-party suspension.
  • Communities live on open relays. Simple, interchangeable servers hold signed copies; if one disappears, you switch.
  • The code is auditable. AGPL-3.0, reproducible builds, public threat model.

We are in alpha. The app works offline, syncs when connectivity returns and sells attention to no one. The full manifesto is on the manifesto page — including the list of things this project will never do.
