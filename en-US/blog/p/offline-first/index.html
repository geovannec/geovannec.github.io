Actually offline-first: what happens when the network drops

2026-10-15 · Engineering

Most apps treat offline as a degraded mode: half the buttons grayed out, a "no connection" banner. In Sovereign Social, offline is the normal state — the network is the exception.

How it works under the hood:

  • Local SQLite with FTS5. Everything you've already seen lives on your computer and is searchable — no server round-trip.
  • Publish queue. Posts written offline stay signed and pending; the relay pool delivers them on reconnect.
  • Reconciliation. Events are identified by hash — different relays converge to the same state without a central coordinator.

The practical consequence: on a highway, on the subway, or when your ISP drops, the feed stays browsable and you keep writing. What you produced is delivered later — carrying your signature, not a server's.
