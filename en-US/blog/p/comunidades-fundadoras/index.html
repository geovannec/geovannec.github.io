Founding communities: growing without an algorithm

2026-11-19 · Community

A good community isn't born from trending topics. It's born from someone inviting the right people.

This project has no discovery algorithm — that's not a limitation, it's a decision. What exists instead:

  • Personal invitation — a community link sent to people you know is worth more than a thousand timeline impressions.
  • Public blog — NIP-23 posts are readable by any Nostr client and linkable from anywhere; it's the community's storefront.
  • Your own relay in 15 minutes — the handbook has the Docker and Raspberry Pi guide; your relay is the definitive copy of the events.

If your community today is hostage to a platform — a WhatsApp group, a subreddit, a forum dying on an old server — the migration tutorial takes an afternoon. Bring the people, not the reach.

It's slower. On purpose.
