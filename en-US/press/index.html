Press

What's inside

The full press kit is at /press/ on this site and in the repository — SVG logo, verifiable fact sheet and boilerplates in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

Free to use in journalism, reviews and mentions — no permission needed.

In the kit

Download directly from /press/ (e.g. /press/fact-sheet.md):

  • logo-mark.svg and logo-lockup.svg — the official brand
  • fact-sheet.md — verifiable facts only (license, stack, status)
  • boilerplate.{pt-BR,en-US,es-419}.md — 'about' texts in 3 languages
  • maintainer-bio.md, screenshots/ and broll/ — pending real human content

Embargo and previews

We respect reasonable embargoes — send date and scope in writing via a 'press' issue on the repository (or security.txt if sensitive).

We don't hand out private builds — press runs exactly the same release binaries users download.

Contact

Open a 'press'-tagged issue at github.com/geovannec/pnet. Sensitive security matters go through the security.txt channel.
