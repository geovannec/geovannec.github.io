Honest comparisons

We're not better at everything — and we say where we lose. The matrix below compares real, documented behavior, not marketing.

Fairness rules

  • We only describe documented public behavior — never rumors.
  • ✗ doesn't mean absolute flaw — it means the feature doesn't exist on that platform today.
  • ~ cells are explained in the notes below — context matters.
  • We don't list features we haven't shipped — no vaporware.
  • The matrix is reviewed every release; if the other side changes, we fix it.

Per-platform notes

Discord

Excellent for real-time chat and voice — a niche we don't compete in. The account is locked to the platform, Terms of Service are global and an entire server can be banned by the company.

Reddit

Huge reach and discovery. Ranking is algorithmic via karma, admins can overrule subreddit moderators, and the 2023 API lockdown showed the risk of depending on the platform.

Facebook Groups

Unbeatable for non-technical audiences. Requires a Facebook account, heavy tracking, algorithmic distribution — and the group can be removed unilaterally.

phpBB / Discourse

Our honest ancestors: open-source and self-hostable. But each forum is a single server — if it dies, the community dies — and the account only exists inside that forum.

Mastodon

Open protocol (ActivityPub) and real federation. The account is tied to the instance — migration exists but depends on the server — and there's no offline-first mode.

Bluesky

Polished UX and federation via AT Protocol. Portability is partial, self-hosting infrastructure is heavy and the project is venture-capital funded.

Other Nostr clients

We're the same family — events interoperate. Most clients focus on microblogging; Sovereign adds forums/communities, wiki, community moderation and governance on top of the same ecosystem.

When not to choose us

Honesty beats conversion. We're not the right choice if:

  • you need real-time voice/video chat — Discord does that very well and we don't;
  • your goal is maximum reach for content marketing — Reddit and Facebook have bigger audiences;
  • you want a hosted community with zero administration — here someone runs the app and chooses relays;
  • losing the account can't be a risk — whoever loses the 24 words loses the identity, and we can't recover it.
