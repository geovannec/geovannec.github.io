Your community. Your keys. Zero ads.

Sovereign Social is a local-first social network: your account is a key that only exists on your device, communities live on open relays, and nothing depends on paid servers.

See the demo Download the app

The problem

Platforms die

Orkut, MySpace, Google+ — entire communities vanished when the server shut down. Here, content is signed and replicated across independent relays: no single shutdown erases everything.

You are the product

Commercial networks sell attention and data. Here there are no ads, no default-on telemetry, no engagement algorithm — the feed is chronological and the filters are yours.

Hostage accounts

Unfair bans, forgotten passwords, a platform deciding for you. In Sovereign the account is a key pair on your computer: nobody suspends it, nobody resets it — and nobody takes it away.

Who it's for

The nostalgic

Miss the forums and scraps of the 2000s? Communities with a name, an owner and their own culture — not an infinite feed.

The sovereigntist

Wants to control their own keys, data and filters. Audits the code, verifies the builds and chooses which relays to trust.

The community owner

Tired of depending on a platform's mood. Here she moderates with her own rules — and the community doesn't die if she switches apps.

What's inside

  • Communities — public forums with topics, wiki and FAQ, published as Nostr events.
  • Scraps and testimonials — profile messages and owner-approved testimonials — as it was, as it should be.
  • Blog and albums — long-form posts (NIP-23) and photo albums with mandatory captions.
  • Community moderation — each community sets its rules, moderators and minimum PoW; nothing is deleted from the network — you filter what you see.
  • Offline-first — everything works without internet and syncs later — local SQLite with FTS5 search.
  • Web of Trust — trust computed from your contact network, not from an opaque algorithm.
  • Importers — bring CSVs from old forums and export your data anytime (takeout).

How it works, jargon-free

  1. When you create your account, your computer generates a key pair. They are your identity — there is no e-mail or password on a server.
  2. You write posts, topics and scraps. Each one gets your digital signature.
  3. Relays — simple public servers — keep signed copies and deliver them to your followers.
  4. You choose which relays to read from and publish to. Everything works offline first; sync happens when the network is back.

Why trust it

  • 100% open auditable code — AGPL-3.0, zero proprietary components in the core.
  • Reproducible builds with SHA256SUMS signed via cosign keyless (Rekor transparency log).
  • Public threat model, security audits and disclosure policy.
  • No venture capital, no ads, no data sales — infrastructure cost is zero by design.
