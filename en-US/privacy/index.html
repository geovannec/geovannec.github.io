Privacy

The principle

The app is local-first: no data leaves your computer without protocol necessity or your explicit action. No analytics, no mandatory telemetry, no trackers, no hidden calls.

This site uses no cookies, no fingerprinting and no third-party analytics. What you read here is what exists in the code.

What public relays see

Events published to public relays are public: content, timestamp and your public key. Treat posts as signed postcards — don't write what can't be public.

Direct messages use NIP-44 (modern encryption). Deletion via NIP-09 is a request relays may honor — not a guarantee that propagated copies are removed.

Your local data

Everything lives in a SQLite on your device, with full export (takeout) anytime. Encryption at rest is available; the panic wipe removes local data in seconds.
