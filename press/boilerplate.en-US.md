# Boilerplate (en-US)

**Short version (50 words):**

> Sovereign Social is a local-first, open-source social network on the
> Nostr protocol: the user's account is a key on their own device,
> communities live on open relays, and nothing depends on paid servers —
> no ads, tracking, or engagement algorithm.

**Long version (~120 words):**

> Sovereign Social is a local-first, open-source (AGPL-3.0) social
> network and forum built on the Nostr protocol. Accounts are key pairs
> generated on the user's device — no e-mail, password, or third-party
> recovery. Communities are public forums with topics, wiki, scraps and
> testimonials, moderated by public community verdicts rather than central
> deletion. Everything works offline and syncs through interchangeable
> open relays. The project is free, with no ads, tracking or telemetry,
> reproducible builds and public security and accessibility documentation.
